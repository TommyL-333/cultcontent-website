Founder's Story

My Story

Before I was a TikTok Shop agency owner, I was a traveling nomad.

For a year, I lived on a converted school bus. I traveled around the country while filming and editing content for product brands.

I saw oceans, mountains, cities, and so many stars...

I experienced a kind of freedom that many people only get to imagine. I was writing my own story, day by day.

Spending so much time in nature taught me to be grounded. Talking with so many interesting people taught me how many different perspectives are out there — and that my perspective is only one of them.

The problems I ran into (propane explosions, heatwaves, questionable hitch-hikers) taught me how to stay calm under pressure.

Living and traveling in the bus was so much fun, and taught me a lot— but most of all it taught me this: you are the creator of your life. You are responsible for making your dreams reality.

Remember that you have the same power, you are the captain of your own ship. It's up to you to set the course and turn the wheel.

How did you get into this industry?

I got my start in social commerce as a UGC creator for product brands.

Within my first year, I generated 100M views from my own videos.

While I was a content creator, TikTok Shop rolled out, and I realized there was more I could do to help my clients.

I began to study affiliate marketing, specifically in the TikTok Shop ecosystem.

It wasn't long after that I started to meet the right people, and in March of 2025, we became official TikTok Shop partners. Over the course of 2025, I developed relationships with the founders of Reacher, Social Army, Social Alchemy, Euka, Cruva, Growi, Sellico, Bullseye Sellers and others.

Why do you do it now?

I do it because it's part of the next step in human evolution, and I want to make sure it goes well. Social commerce is a pivotal shift in the way human beings transact with one another. It's relationship based— but it's also through a screen.

In some ways, commerce becomes more honest, truthful, and beneficial to mankind. In others, it keeps us more isolated than ever before.

As we navigate this new frontier of commerce & community, we must be diligent to do so ethically and to the benefit of the world.

My mission in life is to make dreams come true. Not just my dreams, but all dreams. Through Cult Content, I'm able to help affiliates and CPG brands make their dreams come true. I'm able to help real people fulfill their personal missions, and I'm able to be a part of a broader mission— one that transcends space and time.

Eternally Grateful,
Tommy Lynch
