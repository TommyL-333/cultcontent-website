What to bring

  • Product samples (recommendation: 150–300)
  • A creator onboarding link (QR code to your shop / landing page / Discord)
  • One-page offer sheet (hero products + commission + shipping timing)
  • A team member who can follow up within 48 hours

How to prep

  • Pick 3 hero products creators can film on-site
  • Lock your offer: commission, bundles, coupon, sample policy
  • Plan your follow-up: CRM + message template
  • Show up ready to move fast — this room rewards speed

Important deadlines

Don’t get caught in the fog
