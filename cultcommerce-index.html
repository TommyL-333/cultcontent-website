Common Questions

Answers to what your team will ask.

Do we need to create new content for AppLovin?

No. We repurpose your best-performing TikTok videos directly into AppLovin ad units. If you're already producing short-form content — which you should be — you have everything we need. No new production budget required.

How much time does this require from our team?

One 30-minute strategy call upfront, then creative approvals as we go (usually 15–20 minutes per week). We handle setup, campaign structure, optimization, and reporting. You are not running anything.

Will this cannibalize our Meta or TikTok performance?

No. AppLovin serves ads inside free mobile gaming apps — it's a completely separate inventory pool. You're reaching an audience that doesn't overlap with your Meta or TikTok remarketing lists. It's additive, not competitive.

What happens after 90 days?

You've seen 90 days of real performance data. If it's working, we continue under a standard management agreement. If you want to bring it in-house or move on, we hand you the account with all the data. No lock-in, no exit fees.

We're already running AppLovin internally. Does this still apply?

Possibly — it depends on account status and spend history. Book a call and we'll assess together. We may still be able to unlock additional credits or improve your creative structure.

Why are you offering this for free?

AppLovin's partner program compensates us when we onboard and activate new DTC advertisers. That lets us offer our management and setup at no cost during the trial period. The incentives are aligned: we succeed when your campaigns perform.
