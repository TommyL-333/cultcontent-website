Partner Types

Two ways in.
One network behind you.

Pick the role that fits what you already do. Or fill both.

Sales Partner — Frontend
You close the deal.
We handle everything else.
You have brand relationships. You don't have the execution infrastructure to service omnichannel commerce. We do. Bring us the client and you're on the deal for as long as they stay.
What you do
  • Bring brands into the CC network and own the client relationship
  • Make the introduction, stay in the loop — zero execution required
  • CC and our Service Providers handle all fulfillment, reporting, and billing
Who this is for
  • Creative agencies and brand consultants
  • Amazon agencies looking to add TikTok Shop, Meta, or other channels
  • Media buyers and performance marketers with brand clients
  • Anyone with trusted brand relationships and no desire to build ops
What you earn
20% of all retainers
10% of net sales commission
On every deal you refer, for as long as that client is active. No cap, no expiry.
Service Provider — Backend
You execute one channel
exceptionally well.
You're great at what you do — TikTok Shop, Meta, Amazon, SEO, whatever your lane is. You just don't want to build a sales team to fill your pipeline. We route you qualified, contracted clients.
What you do
  • Deliver your channel service at the standard CC clients expect
  • Operate within the CC network — we handle contracts, billing, and client comms
  • Focus entirely on execution — no selling, no chasing invoices
Who this is for
  • Channel specialists with proven execution (TikTok Shop, Meta, Amazon, etc.)
  • Small agencies that are great at delivery but struggle with acquisition
  • Freelancers and consultants who want consistent, qualified deal flow
What you earn
100% when you bring the deal
70% on CC-referred deals
Retainer + net sales share for your channel, every month the client is active. The 30% on referred deals covers CC's network orchestration, billing, and sales overhead.
Partners can fill both roles simultaneously. If you close a deal and also execute one of the channel services on it, you earn on both sides — the referral comp and the service delivery comp.

Why Join

What you get by being
inside the network.

Going it alone means building the sales function, the ops layer, the contracts, and the billing infrastructure yourself. Or you plug into what's already built.

01
Qualified deal flow — no chasing
CC runs the sales and marketing. Service Providers get clients routed to them who are already contracted, briefed, and ready to start. No cold outreach. No tire kickers.
02
Omnichannel without building every channel
Sales Partners can pitch the full omnichannel stack — TikTok Shop, Meta, Amazon, SEO, and more — without having execution capacity for any of it. The network covers it.
03
Contracts, billing, and ops handled
CC manages the client agreement, the invoicing, the commission tracking, and the reporting infrastructure. Partners focus on what they're good at — not the overhead.
04
Revenue that compounds
Referral comp runs for the life of the client, not just the first month. Build a book of introductions and you're earning on all of them every month — without managing any of them.
05
Early position in a fast-moving network
The partners who come in now help define how services get priced, scoped, and positioned as the network scales. This is a founding partner relationship — not a vendor signup.
06
Built on technology, not headcount
CC's platform handles creator sourcing, campaign management, client portals, and reporting programmatically. The margin that would go to overhead goes to partners instead.
