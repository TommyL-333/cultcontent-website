Synopsis

Bora Mutluoglu, co-founder of Reacher and Y Combinator W25 alum, joins Tommy Lynch for the inaugural episode of the Cult Commerce Podcast. Bora built Reacher from a $500/month Python automation script into one of the leading TikTok Shop AI platforms — and he did it while watching the entire TikTok Shop landscape transform underneath him.

This conversation covers the full arc: from the gold rush days when mass-sampling guaranteed sales, to the current reality where established brands deliberately lose money on TikTok to capture Amazon halo revenue, to the near future where two-person companies run agentic AI teams that do the work of ten.

The centerpiece of the episode is a live walkthrough of what an actual agentic social commerce workflow looks like — using Claude connected to the Reacher API to find competitors, analyze their top-performing content, generate creator outreach, and automate follow-up daily, entirely hands-off. No hypotheticals. This is real infrastructure that agencies are running today.

Key Takeaways
🚫
Mass sampling is dead.
The TikTok subsidies that made "send samples to everyone" work in 2023 are gone. Cold-starting a brand today requires finding 5–10 creators who genuinely love the product first — by hand, at events, through DMs — before scaling outreach.
📦
The 40% Amazon Halo Effect is real — and it changes everything.
Big brands intentionally break even on TikTok Shop because a viral video lifts their Amazon and Shopify revenue by 40%. For them, TikTok is a marketing channel, not a profit center. This is the competitor you're up against — plan accordingly.
✍️
The "write" unlock is what separates AI users from AI operators.
Most brands use AI in "read" mode — summarize, analyze, report. The actual economic leverage is "write" mode: agents that take action. Finding creators → sending outreach → booking samples → sending briefs → doing it every day, automatically.
🤖
One Claude prompt can replace a full-time role.
The creatine gummy workflow Bora describes — competitor analysis, creator search, outreach, sample booking, brief delivery, daily repeat — is a single connected agent instruction. Set it once and it runs forever. This is not the future. Agencies are doing it today.
🌐
Omnichannel is no longer optional.
TikTok Shop + Amazon + Shopify is the minimum viable stack. Meta Shopping, YouTube Shopping, and Amazon Affiliate are all moving fast. Brands that build only on TikTok will be competing against companies that capture the halo across every channel.
🔮
In 5 years: two-person brands, ten-person output.
Bora's vision: AI-native brands run by a founder and a product person, with agents handling every platform — outreach, ads, retention, Amazon, Shopify. The headcount question changes from "how many people do I need?" to "which agents do I deploy?"
