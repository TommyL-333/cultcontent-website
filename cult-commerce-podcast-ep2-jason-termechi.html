The Live Commerce Playbook ft. Jason Termechi

With Jason Termechi, Former TikTok Live Account Manager  ·  Hosted by Tommy Lynch

Spotify Apple Podcasts YouTube
Synopsis

Jason Termechi spent 1.5 years as a TikTok Live Account Manager, working directly with 30+ brands and generating over $14M in GMV through live commerce. He even traveled to China to study Douyin's live ecosystem firsthand. Now he's consulting on live strategy and building Get Live Hosts — a marketplace connecting brands with vetted live hosts by geography, skill set, and rate.

In this episode, Jason breaks down the full live commerce playbook: which products are actually live-ready (and the five qualifiers that determine it), why founder-led live outperforms agency-managed shows, and how the affiliate live model is the fastest path for creators to generate income without inventory risk.

The centerpiece of the conversation is what China has already proven: 70% of purchases on Douyin happen via live stream, with over $1B in affiliate GMV. The playbook exists. Jason walked it. Here's what's coming to the US — and how to get ahead of it with the crawl/walk/run framework before the window closes.

Key Takeaways
🎯
Not every product is live-ready — here are the qualifiers.
Live commerce works best for products that can be demonstrated, have a compelling story, and hit a specific price range that feels like an impulsive purchase. If your product can't be shown in action or explained in 60 seconds, live is a hard road. Jason's five-point qualifier framework is the first filter every brand should run before investing in live.
👨‍💼
Founder-led live outperforms agency-managed, every time.
Authenticity is the unfair advantage in live commerce. When a founder goes live and can speak genuinely to why they built the product, conversion rates consistently beat polished host performances. Jason saw this pattern repeatedly across his brand portfolio — the most credible person is usually the one who invented the thing.
🤝
Affiliate live is the entry point for most creators.
Creators don't need their own product to generate income from live. The affiliate live model — hosting brands' products on a commission basis — is both the lowest-friction entry point and the fastest path to meaningful revenue. For brands, it's also the safest way to test live before committing to a full production infrastructure.
🇨🇳
China already solved live commerce — the playbook exists.
70% of purchases on Douyin happen via live stream. The affiliate GMV in China has crossed $1B. Jason visited China specifically to study this ecosystem and came back with a clear picture of where US live commerce is heading. The question isn't whether this comes here — it's whether you're positioned when it does.
📐
Crawl/walk/run: how to start without burning budget.
Most brands try to run before they can walk in live — over-investing in production quality before proving the concept. Jason's crawl/walk/run framework gives a clear sequence: start with the founder on a phone, prove conversion, then invest in hosts and production. Skipping stages is the most common and costly mistake.
🔮
In 5 years: live commerce is a standard channel like paid search.
Just as every brand budgets for Google Ads today, every brand will budget for live in five years. The brands building live muscle now — audience, hosts, production, format — will own distribution when the channel matures. The window to be an early mover is closing faster than most people realize.
