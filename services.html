Service FAQs

Common questions about our services and approach

How quickly can I expect results?

Most clients see initial sales within the first week, with significant traction by day 30. Our affiliate network typically generates consistent daily sales by week 3.

Do you work with all product types?

We work best with products that have visual appeal and solve real problems. Fashion, beauty, home goods, and innovative gadgets typically perform exceptionally well.

What's included in content creation?

Everything from concept to final edit – scripting, filming, editing, trending audio selection, captions, and hashtag research. All optimized for TikTok Shop conversion.

How do you find affiliates?

We use Euka AI combined with our proprietary database of 10,000+ creators. We match affiliates based on audience fit, engagement rates, and past performance.

What ad budget do you recommend?

We recommend starting with $5,000/month for testing, then scaling based on ROAS. Most successful clients invest $10,000-50,000/month in ads after finding winning creatives.

Can I upgrade my package?

Absolutely! Most clients start with Starter or Growth and upgrade as they see results. We'll recommend the optimal time to scale based on your performance data.
