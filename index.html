Frequently Asked
Questions

How fast can we launch?
+
Most brands are live within 3–7 days from onboarding. The Foundation is a 7-day delivery. Full service management begins with a 2-week prelaunch phase to set up correctly before we drive traffic.
Do you run affiliate outreach for us?
+
Yes — affiliate outreach, sampling campaigns, TAP campaign registration, and creator management are core parts of what we do. We don't hand you a strategy doc. We build the infrastructure and run it.
Do we need discounts to win?
+
During launch, yes — you're paying the cost of momentum. Higher affiliate commissions and strategic discounts are how you capture early traction. We model this into your unit economics upfront.
What does success look like?
+
We've taken 4 shops from $0 to 5 figures/month. The benchmark we work toward: a predictable content engine, an active affiliate network, and a shop performance score that compounds week over week.
Who is this for?
+
E-commerce brands serious about TikTok Shop. If you're earlier stage, The Foundation is built for you. If you want full management, we'll talk through fit on the call.
