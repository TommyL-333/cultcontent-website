Partner contracts need a data clause

Creator communities join the network for supply and data together. Without a data-sharing term, their creators never reach the Creator Graph.

Half of Commerce is still a report

Amazon, Walmart, AI UGC and fulfillment arrive as partner reports, not feeds. Each one is a dollar the ledger cannot see until the partner exposes an API or a shared export.

Licensing is the one new stream

The likeness registry is the only stream that does not exist anywhere yet. It feeds all three layers: who licensed, which videos used them, and what royalties are owed.
