The Honest Truth

TikTok Shop rewards
brands that fund it properly.

We were part of the team that helped build $30 million in TikTok Shop revenue in a single year. Not for one brand — across four brands, each taken from zero to five figures a month. The platform works. The question is whether you're running the playbook that actually makes it work, or a stripped-down version of it that looks right on paper and underdelivers in practice.

Here's what we've seen over and over: brands come in with a real product and real demand, and they hire an agency that knows the right words — affiliates, retainers, GMV Max, live strategy. The problem isn't the strategy. It's that none of the pieces are actually funded well enough to work, and there's no dedicated person running the day-to-day. 30 samples go out and nothing happens. An account manager who juggles 20 other clients "checks in" weekly. The ads go live before the organic flywheel is moving. Month three, the brand is frustrated and the agency has an excuse.

The Scale package exists because we know exactly what threshold each piece of this needs to cross before it starts compounding.

Creator retainers need to be real commitments, not $500 gifting deals. Sampling needs to move at 300+ per month, not 30. Live strategy needs to be an actual consultation service — flash sales, scripts, packages, training — not an improvised afterthought. And the person coordinating all of it needs to know your product catalog cold and talk to your creators every single week — not as a side responsibility, but as their entire job. That’s what the Brand Concierge is. Your dedicated point of contact on our team, sourced from the creator community, embedded in your brand — not shared across a client roster.
