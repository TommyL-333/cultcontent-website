The Real Problem

TikTok Shop Isn’t
a Free Channel.

Brands come in expecting organic magic. They send 20 samples, wait 60 days, and wonder why nothing happened. Here’s what actually happens when you underfund it.

No real creator commitment

Without paid retainers, creators post when they feel like it. You get one video, maybe two, and then silence. There’s no algorithm signal, no momentum, no reason for the platform to push your product.

Too few samples, too slow

You need 300+ samples moving per month to generate enough content volume to find what converts. 20 samples tells you almost nothing. It just means 20 creators who may or may not post.

No live strategy

TikTok LIVE is one of the highest-converting surfaces on the platform. Brands that skip it leave a massive revenue channel untouched — and their competitors aren’t skipping it.

Waiting for organic before running ads

Paid amplification needs something to amplify. But waiting until everything is “working” before spending means you’re always three months behind where you could be.
