Partner Network

Five ways to
partner with us.

You can apply for more than one. Most partners end up holding two or three of these at once — pick whatever actually describes what you do.

Sales You bring brands in

You refer a brand into the network. When they sign on and start paying, you earn 20% of their monthly retainer and 10% of net sales, for as long as they stay a client — on every service they buy, regardless of who executes the work.

Who this is forAgency owners, consultants, and operators with warm relationships to brands doing $10M+ in revenue who need omnichannel help.
Service Provider You execute the work

You're assigned to run a channel — TikTok Shop, Amazon, Meta, Google, Shopify — for a client. If you brought the client in yourself, you keep 100% of that service's retainer and net sales. If a Sales partner referred them, you keep the split set for that assignment (70% by default).

Who this is forSpecialist agencies and operators who deliver real channel execution — paid media, marketplace management, affiliate programs — and want client flow without running their own sales funnel.
Consultant You sell your own time

You get a public, bookable profile — bio, specialties, your own rate — where brands pay directly to book 15/30/60-minute paid sessions with you. This is separate from Service Provider: you're not assigned ongoing client work, you're selling your own expertise by the hour.

Who this is forOperators with a specific, teachable expertise — TikTok Shop strategy, creator management, live shopping — who want paid consulting calls without taking on full account management.
Software You provide a tool

You build a tool or platform that plugs into how the network operates — something brands or other partners actually use day to day, not just a listing. Terms here are worked out directly with our team since every software partnership looks different.

Who this is forTeams behind a tool relevant to TikTok Shop, creator management, attribution, or e-commerce operations that our brands or partners would genuinely use.
Mission You're aligned on the bigger goal

You're an organization working toward the same thing we are — a creator economy that actually works for the people in it. Mission partnerships aren't primarily commercial; they're about shared infrastructure, shared creators, and shared advocacy.

Who this is forNonprofits, creator advocacy groups, and mission-driven organizations in the creator-commerce space.

Most real partnerships are a combination — a Sales partner who's also a Service Provider for one channel, or a Consultant who's also aligned on Mission. Select everything that applies on the application; we'll sort out the specifics together.
